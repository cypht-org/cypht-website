## Search & Filter Capabilities {#overview}

Cypht provides search and filtering functionality to help you find and organize emails across your accounts.

:::grid
- **Search Across Accounts**
  Search all your email accounts and RSS feeds with simple forms or advanced search modules
:::

## Unified Search {#unified_search}

Search across all your configured email accounts and RSS feeds simultaneously.

### Simple Search Form {#simple_search}

:::grid
- **Search All Accounts**
  Search all your E-mail accounts and RSS feeds at once with a simple form.
:::

### Advanced Search Module {#advanced_search}

:::grid
- **Complex Searches**
  Do complex searches across your accounts with the advanced search module set.
:::

## Saved Search Parameters {#saved_searches}

Save frequently used search parameters for quick access later.

:::grid
- **Save and Access Searches**
  Save the parameters of a search so that you can quickly access them later from the menu without having to enter them
  again. This is particularly useful for parameters of searches that are used frequently. Saved search parameters can
  also be deleted later.
:::

## Sieve Filters {#sieve_filters}

Server-side email filtering to automatically organize your inbox.

:::grid
- **Automatic Email Organization**
  Sieve filters can be set up to automatically move, copy, or delete messages based on specific criteria such as
  sender, subject, keywords, or recipient. This allows for efficient organization and management of incoming emails,
  saving time and improving productivity.
:::

### Setting Up Sieve Filters {#sieve_setup}

:::grid
- **Add Filter Process**
  To add a Sieve filter, navigate to the Sieve Filters menu located in the Settings tab sidebar, select the email
  account for which you want to create a filter, and click the "Add Filter" button.
- **Filter Management**
  Sieve filters can be edited, deleted, and extended with custom scripts. For step-by-step setup instructions,
  examples, and troubleshooting, use the dedicated Sieve guide.
:::
