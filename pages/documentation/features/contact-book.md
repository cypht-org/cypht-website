## Contact Management {#overview}

Cypht includes a one-click Contact Book that allows you to efficiently add people to your contacts, making it easy to
manage your email connections.

:::grid
- **Easy Contact Management**
  Efficiently add people to your contacts with one-click functionality
:::

## Adding Contacts {#adding_contacts}

The Contact Book provides multiple convenient ways to add new contacts to your address book.

### Main Menu Access {#main_menu_access}

:::grid
- **Contacts Button**
  Contacts can be added via clicking the Contacts button from the main menu.
:::

### Direct from Message {#message_access}

:::grid
- **One-Person Icon**
  Or simply by clicking on the "one-person" icon in the message you receive.
:::

## Contact Benefits {#contact_benefits}

Discover how the Contact Book improves your email workflow.

The one-click Contact Book feature makes it effortless to build and maintain your network of contacts. By providing
quick access to contact creation directly from the main menu or from received messages, Cypht ensures you can easily
save important email addresses and contact information without interrupting your workflow. This efficient system helps
you stay organized and maintain better communication with your contacts.
