## Meet Cypht {#meet_cypht}

Cypht is a **modern, Open Source webmail client** that brings together multiple email accounts into one secure,
unified dashboard. Designed for speed, privacy, and flexibility. Cypht lets you take full control of your email
experience.

## Why Cypht? {#why_cypht}

Cypht is a revolutionary webmail client that transforms how you manage your email. Unlike traditional email clients,
Cypht is designed from the ground up to be secure, private, and incredibly powerful.

Built with modern web technologies and a focus on user privacy, Cypht allows you to :

- Connect unlimited email accounts from any provider
- Manage all your emails from a single, unified interface
- Keep your data secure with self-hosted deployment
- Enjoy advanced features like smart filtering and encryption
- Customize your experience with themes and layouts

## Security & Data Protection {#security_privacy}

Your data privacy is our absolute priority. Cypht is designed with advanced security protocols to protect your
communications.

Self-Hosted
: Take complete control of your data by hosting Cypht on your own servers.

Open Source
: Complete transparency with accessible and verifiable code for everyone.

Zero Data Collection
: Your emails stay on your servers, with no spying or data analysis.

Advanced Encryption
: Optimal protection with TLS/SSL protocols and integrated PGP support.

Authentication Options
: Support for multiple authentication methods to ensure secure access.
