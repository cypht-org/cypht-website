      <div id="why_cypht"  class="doc-section">
        <div class="doc-section-header">
            <a href="#why_cypht">Why Cypht?</a>
        </div>
          <p>Cypht is a revolutionary webmail client that transforms how you manage your email. Unlike traditional email clients, Cypht is designed from the ground up to be secure, private, and incredibly powerful.</p>
          <p>Built with modern web technologies and a focus on user privacy, Cypht allows you to:</p>
          <!--  -->
          <ul>
            <li>Connect unlimited email accounts from any provider</li>
            <li>Manage all your emails from a single, unified interface</li>
            <li>Keep your data secure with self-hosted deployment</li>
            <li>Enjoy advanced features like smart filtering and encryption</li>
            <li>Customize your experience with themes and layouts</li>
          </ul>
            <!--  -->
          <span class="doc-section-text">
