# Email Scheduling

Email scheduling allows you to compose emails and send them at a specific time in the future, helping you manage your communication more effectively.

## What is Email Scheduling?

Email scheduling is a feature that:

- Allows you to compose emails now and send them later
- Helps you maintain consistent communication
- Enables you to work across different time zones
- Provides better control over your email workflow

## Scheduling Features

### Basic Scheduling

- **Send Later**: Schedule emails for specific times
- **Date Picker**: Choose specific dates
- **Time Picker**: Choose specific times
- **Time Zone**: Handle different time zones

### Advanced Scheduling

- **Recurring Emails**: Schedule recurring messages
- **Bulk Scheduling**: Schedule multiple emails
- **Template Scheduling**: Schedule from templates
- **Conditional Scheduling**: Schedule based on conditions

## Scheduling Interface

### Compose with Scheduling

1. Compose your email
