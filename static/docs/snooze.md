# Snooze

The Snooze feature allows you to temporarily hide emails and bring them back at a later time, helping you manage your inbox more effectively.

## What is Snooze?

Snooze is a productivity feature that lets you:

- Temporarily remove emails from your inbox
- Set a specific time for emails to return
- Focus on important emails without distractions
- Manage your email workflow more effectively

## How Snooze Works

### Basic Snooze Process

1. Select an email to snooze
2. Choose when to bring it back
3. Email disappears from inbox
4. Email returns at specified time
5. Continue working with the email

### Snooze Options

- **Later Today**: Bring back later in the day
- **Tomorrow**: Bring back tomorrow
- **This Weekend**: Bring back on weekend
- **Next Week**: Bring back next week
- **Custom Time**: Set specific date and time
